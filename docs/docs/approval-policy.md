SNote Approval Policy

Purpose

This policy defines how SNote handles human approval before a system or product can receive official SOLIDMOVE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED product status.

Core Rule

«Technical verification is not human approval.»

A repository, deployment, Pyramide verification, DPY Core verification, SNote verification, documentation, or AI recommendation does not automatically constitute company approval.

Approval Requirements

Before an item can become an official SolidMove product, the applicable requirements must be satisfied:

1. Ownership is established.
2. Company policy requirements are satisfied.
3. Applicable country/regulatory requirements are reviewed.
4. Technical evidence is sufficient.
5. Required Pyramide/DPY Core verification is completed.
6. SNote evidence verification is completed.
7. The approval scope is defined.
8. An authorized human explicitly approves the item.

Approval Status

Pending

Approval has not yet been granted.

Official SolidMove Product: No.

Approved

An authorized human has explicitly approved the exact item, version, and defined scope.

Approval must be supported by a verifiable approval record.

Rejected

An authorized human has explicitly rejected the item.

Official SolidMove Product: No.

Approval Scope

Approval applies only to the scope explicitly recorded.

The record should identify, where applicable:

- Product or system
- Version
- Repository
- Deployment
- Features
- Environment
- Ownership
- Geographic or regulatory scope
- Commercial limitations
- Security limitations
- Approval date
- Review date

Approval of one item does not automatically approve connected systems, products, future versions, or unrelated components.

Explicit Human Approval

The approval record must identify:

- Approver
- Role
- Date
- Item
- Version
- Scope
- Evidence reviewed
- Approval decision
- Approval reference
- Verifiable approval evidence

For official SolidMove product recognition, authorized human approval must be explicit.

No Implied Approval

Approval must never be inferred from:

- GitHub or GitLab ownership
- A commit
- A repository
- A deployment
- Successful testing
- Pyramide
- DPY Core
- SNote verification
- AI output
- Documentation
- Marketing material
- Company ownership alone

Approval and Negative Control

If required approval is missing, SNote keeps the item in a non-official state.

Re-approval

A new review may be required when there is a material change to:

- Product architecture
- Ownership
- Major version
- Production deployment
- Security model
- Regulatory scope
- Commercial purpose
- Material functionality

Previous approval must not be assumed to cover material changes.

Public Repository Safety

Never publish private approval credentials, signatures, authentication tokens, private contracts, or other confidential evidence in this public repository.

Public records should reference controlled evidence safely.

Final Rule

«No explicit authorized human approval means no official SolidMove product status.»

SNote records the approval decision and evidence. It does not manufacture the decision.
