## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the legal, regulatory, and operational baseline for SolidMove Technology Limited (“SolidMove”, “the Company”) and its Trust Network (STN). It defines the governance rules for data protection, KYC compliance, risk assessment, and secure technical operations within the Federal Republic of Nigeria. It is binding on all employees, contractors, partners, and systems operating under the SolidMove brand.

---

## 2. Legal Framework

### 2.1 Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020
SolidMove Technology Limited is incorporated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with registration number **RC: 9619709**. All corporate activities, contracts, and governance structures comply with CAMA 2020.

### 2.2 Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) / NDPA
The Company processes personal data in accordance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)** and the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)**. Key principles include:
- Lawful, fair, and transparent processing.
- Purpose limitation and data minimisation.
- Storage limitation and integrity.
- Confidentiality and security of personal data.

### 2.3 Other Applicable Regulations
- **CBN Guidelines** (where financial services are offered).
- **Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015**.
- **Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020**.
- Any other applicable Nigerian and international regulations as the Company scales.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Privacy (NDPR Compliance)

### 3.1 Data Collection
Personal data is collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes. Data subjects are informed of the purpose of collection and their rights.

### 3.2 Data Subject Rights
SolidMove upholds the rights of data subjects, including:
- Right to be informed.
- Right of access.
- Right to rectification.
- Right to erasure (right to be forgotten).
- Right to restrict processing.
- Right to data portability.
- Right to object.

### 3.3 Data Security
All personal data is protected using industry-standard security measures, including encryption, access controls, and regular audits. Data is stored on secure servers with SSL/TLS encryption.

### 3.4 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach, SolidMove will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and affected data subjects within the timeframe required by law.

## 4. Know Your Customer (KYC) & Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

### 4.1 Level-3 (L3) KYC Standards
SolidMove adheres to **Level-3 (L3) KYC** standards for user and partner verification. This includes:
- **Identity Verification:** Government-issued ID, biometric verification, and liveness checks.
- **Address Verification:** Utility bills, bank statements, or official correspondence.
- **Source of Funds/Wealth:** Documentation for high-value transactions.
- **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD):** For politically exposed persons (PEPs) and high-risk jurisdictions.

### 4.2 AML Policy
The Company maintains an AML policy that includes:
- Transaction monitoring.
- Suspicious activity reporting.
- Record-keeping for a minimum of 5 years.
- Regular staff training on AML compliance.

### 4.3 Risk Assessment
All customers and partners are subject to risk-based assessment using the **OSA Risk & Velocity Assessment Pipeline**. The pipeline evaluates:
- **Country risk** (FATF lists, sanctions).
- **Customer risk** (profile, activity).
- **Product/Service risk**.
- **Delivery channel risk**.

High-risk entities require additional due diligence and may be denied service.

## 5. Trust Network (STN) Governance

The SolidMove Trust Network (STN) is the ecosystem of partners, validators, and infrastructure providers that operate under the SolidMove governance framework.

### 5.1 Membership & Eligibility
Membership is granted only to entities that:
- Pass L3 KYC and AML screening.
- Agree to the SolidMove Code of Conduct.
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
- Successfully pass technical and security audits.

### 5.2 Governance Structure
- **Founder & CEO:** Final decision-making authority.
- **Compliance Officer:** Ensures regulatory adherence.
- **Technical Audit Team:** Verifies security and performance.
- **Dispute Resolution Committee:** Handles conflicts.

### 5.3 Dispute Resolution
Disputes are resolved through:
1. Negotiation between parties.
2. Mediation by a neutral third party.
3. Arbitration in accordance with Nigerian law.

## 6. Domain & Technical Governance

### 6.1 Official Domain
The official domain of SolidMove is **solidmovetech.com**. All web traffic and corporate communications must use this domain. SSL/TLS encryption is enforced for all connections.

### 6.2 Origin Isolation
The Pyramide DPY backend (dpy-core-api) enforces **CORS origin isolation**. Only whitelisted origins (e.g., the corporate web portal) may access backend resources. All other requests are blocked.

### 6.3 Infrastructure Security
- Hosted on **Render** with automated SSL/TLS.
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scans.
- Immutable logging to the **Solidmove Trust Vault (🌑 selene-core)** for all critical operations.

### 6.4 Telemetry & Monitoring
- Live HTTP status checks for all public endpoints.
- CORS policy enforcement monitoring.
- Alerting for anomalies.
